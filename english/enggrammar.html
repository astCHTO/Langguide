English Grammar Guide

Pronouns

English is an West Germanic language that was first spoken in early medieval England. It has a verbal syntagma SVO (subject-verb-object) a nominal syntagma DAN (determiner-adjective-noun). It has no grammatical gender and does not have a complex system of grammatical case, its cases are just present today in the pronouns and the genitive case (adding a "'s" in the end of the noun).

Verbs

The verbs in English were very simplificated over the centuries. Currently, English has a relatively simple verb system compared to many other languages. And the unique verb that keeps conjugating in all persons is the verb "to be". The rest just preserve a conjugation in the 3rd person singular.
