German Grammar Guide

German is a West Germanic language that is spoken in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and even in Brazil (the second most spoken language there). It has a verbal syntagma V2 (Verb in the second position) and somethimes can also be a SVOV (when a auxiliary verb is used the main verb goes to the end of the sentence) a nominal syntagma DAN (determiner-adjective-noun). It has a complex system of grammatical cases, with three grammatical genders and four main cases: Nominative, Accusative, Dative, and Genitive.

Verbs

The verbs in German are highly inflected, with each verb having multiple forms depending on the subject and tense, But they usually have a very intuitive conjugation pattern.
