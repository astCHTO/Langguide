Portuguese Grammar Guide

Portuguese is a Ibero-Romance language spoken in Brazil, Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, and other Lusophone countries. It typically follows an SVO word order (Subject–Verb–Object) and a DNA nominal syntagma (Determiner–Noun–Adjective, where adjectives usually follow the noun). Portuguese does not have grammatical cases on its nouns, relying instead on prepositions and word order, while marking grammatical gender (masculine/feminine) and number (singular/plural) across nouns, articles, pronouns, and adjectives.

Verbs

Portuguese verbs inflect for tense, mood, person, and number, and distinguish between permanent or inherent traits (ser) and temporary states, locations, or changes (estar).
