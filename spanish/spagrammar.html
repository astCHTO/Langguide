Spanish Grammar Guide

Spanish is an Ibero-Romance language spoken in Spain, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, and across most of Latin America. It typically follows an SVO word order (Subject–Verb–Object) and a DNA nominal syntagma (Determiner–Noun–Adjective, where adjectives usually follow the noun). Spanish does not have grammatical cases on its nouns, relying instead on prepositions and word order, while marking grammatical gender (masculine/feminine) and number (singular/plural) across nouns, articles, pronouns, and adjectives.

Verbs

Spanish verbs inflect for tense, mood, person, and number, and distinguish between permanent or inherent traits (ser) and temporary states, locations, or results (estar).
